# "Good lord, that thing is stupid."

Jellyshades are notorious for opening their fights with an attack known within their AI as "Hurling". They also have a hive mind in a way, where one is declared a leader based on attack order.

This mod effectively adds a 65-frame timer after attack eligiblity to prevent them from Hurling into the player without warning. If the leader is declaring a Hurling attack but no other Jellyshades are eligible for the attack yet, the declaration is ignored until all current in-combat Jellyshades are eligible to perform Hurling. All other attacks and all other vanilla behaviors are the same as expected, so this is just a win all around with no change to decision making or attack patterns by the enemy, making this mod a Vanilla+ QoL addition.
